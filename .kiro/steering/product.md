# Product Overview

Enterprise talent acquisition platform for planning and managing recruitment events (Hackathons, Bootcamps, Networking events).

## Core Capabilities

- Event planning and management for recruitment activities
- Venue tracking with status history and capacity management
- Company profile management including tech stack and hiring needs
- University and student club relationship management
- Candidate sourcing and pre-screening workflows
- Skills gap analysis between company needs and candidate pools
- Task management with dependencies for event coordination
- Automated reminder system for follow-ups

## Target Users

- Talent acquisition teams
- University relations coordinators
- Event planners
- Recruitment managers
